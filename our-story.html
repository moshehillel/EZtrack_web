Our Story

From a simple idea to revolutionizing fleet management

The Beginning

EZ Track was founded in 2018 by a team of logistics professionals who experienced firsthand the challenges of managing vehicle fleets without proper tracking technology. Our founders, Sarah Chen and Michael Rodriguez, combined their expertise in logistics and software development to create a solution that would transform how businesses monitor their vehicles.

Our Mission

At EZ Track, we're on a mission to make vehicle tracking accessible, affordable, and effective for businesses of all sizes. We believe that every fleet operator deserves real-time visibility into their operations, whether they're managing a single delivery van or a fleet of hundreds of trucks.

The Journey

What started as a small garage operation in Brooklyn has grown into a trusted partner for over 5,000 businesses nationwide. Our journey has been marked by continuous innovation, customer-centric development, and an unwavering commitment to reliability.
